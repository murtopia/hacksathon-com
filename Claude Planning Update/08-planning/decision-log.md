# Decision Log

**Status:** Living

## 2026-06-28

### Positioning

- Working category is Organizational AI Adoption.
- Hacks-a-Thon helps organizations accelerate AI adoption by empowering every employee to confidently create solutions.
- The homepage should lead with AI adoption and human transformation, not the hackathon format.

### Mission and Vision

- Mission: We help people realize they can create solutions they never thought possible.
- Vision: We envision organizations where every employee feels empowered to create solutions.

### Language

- Use create more often than build where possible.
- Prefer tools over software when referring to what people create.
- Use superpowers sparingly, but keep it as authentic founder language.
- AI should be described as a creative partner.

### Culture and Team Building

- Team building is better framed as culture building through shared creation.
- Fun is part of the methodology.
- Shark Tank-style presentations are a culture-building mechanism.
- Shared creation leads to shared accomplishment. Shared accomplishment builds culture. Culture accelerates AI adoption.

### Website

- Primary CTA direction should be self-service.
- Preferred CTA language: See How It Works.
- Interactive demos should become a key conversion path.

### Research

- Participant reflections validate that people overestimate how difficult AI creation will be.
- Participant reflections validate that fun, ownership, and confidence are central to the experience.
- Market conversations show people are overwhelmed by AI noise and unsure who to trust.
