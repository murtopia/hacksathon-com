# Core Beliefs

Status: Approved v1.0

## Organizations don't adopt AI. People do.

Technology doesn't transform organizations. People do.

## AI adoption isn't something you roll out. It's something you cultivate.

Policies, software, and training matter. But lasting adoption grows through confidence, experimentation, collaboration, and repeated success.

## People become confident by creating.

Watching builds awareness. Creating something meaningful builds confidence.

## Every employee can become a creator.

AI has changed who gets to create solutions. You no longer need to be a software engineer to solve problems, automate work, or bring ideas to life.

## Innovation already exists inside the organization.

The people closest to the work are often the first to recognize opportunities for improvement. They need confidence, structure, and permission to create.
