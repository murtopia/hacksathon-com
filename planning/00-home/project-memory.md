## Website Structure To Protect

The site should remain focused and self-service.

Primary structure:

1. Home
2. Platform
3. Solutions
4. Pricing
5. Resources
6. About / Case Study

Notes:

- Do not overcomplicate the architecture early.
- Solutions should become the home for vertical landing pages.
- At launch, Solutions should include a general company page and an agency-specific page.
- The agency landing page is the first vertical because small ad agencies are the initial go-to-market focus.
- The homepage should sell the value and transformation, not explain every feature.
- The platform page should explain the admin and participant experiences, including the interactive demos.
- Pricing should make the decision feel easy without underselling the value.

## Ideas To Protect

- The culture hack: the participant experience creates the team-building and culture-building outcome.
- Two-layer model: executives buy AI adoption and culture; participants experience fun, creativity, ownership, confidence, and shared accomplishment.
- The participant experience creates the executive outcome.
- AI is a creative partner.
- The best ideas still begin with people.
- Culture is built through shared creation.
- Shared creation leads to shared accomplishment. Shared accomplishment builds culture. Culture accelerates AI adoption.
- People do not need more AI noise. They need a clear place to start.
- The first product is Hacks-a-Thon, but the larger company may become an Organizational AI Adoption platform.
