# Writing Style Guide

**Status:** Living

## Voice

- Direct
- Conversational
- Executive
- Human
- Optimistic without hype
- Clear over clever

## Writing Rules

- Do not use em dashes.
- Prefer short paragraphs.
- Prefer active voice.
- Avoid unnecessary buzzwords.
- Meet the reader where they are before introducing our philosophy.

## Phrases to Avoid

These phrases are not banned, but should be intentionally reviewed before using because they often feel AI-generated or overused.

- Here's the thing
- Let that sink in
- The hidden tax
- The gap between
- It's not X, it's Y
- No X No Y Just Z
- The quiet shift
- But here's the kicker
- Make no mistake
- This changes everything
- Let's unpack this
- Dive deep into
- A masterclass in
- The future is here
- In today's landscape
- Unlock your potential
- Read that again
- Plot twist
- Stop doing X
- Harness the power of
- Elevate your game
- What if I told you
- The takeaway
- Buckle up
- Game changer
- At the end of the day
- Needle moving
- Move the needle
- Low hanging fruit
- Best in class
- Double edged sword
- The bottom line
