# Hacks-a-Thon Flywheel

**Status:** Locked  
**Version:** 1.0

## Summary

The Hacks-a-Thon Flywheel explains why the experience creates both AI adoption and culture.

The core mechanism is shared creation.

When people create something meaningful, share it with others, and experience accomplishment together, they build confidence. That confidence spreads through the organization and accelerates AI adoption.

## The Flywheel

1. People create something meaningful.
2. They surprise themselves.
3. Confidence grows.
4. They share what they made.
5. Other people start asking, "What could I create?"
6. People create more.
7. Culture changes.
8. AI adoption accelerates.

## Why It Matters

Traditional team building often creates shared activity.

Hacks-a-Thon creates shared accomplishment.

That difference matters.

People bond differently when they struggle, laugh, create, and present something together.

The result is not just a fun event. It is a cultural shift where people begin talking collaboratively about what they can create using AI.

## Core Thesis

Shared creation leads to shared accomplishment.

Shared accomplishment builds culture.

Culture accelerates AI adoption.
