# Market Notes

**Status:** Research  
**Purpose:** Capture outside observations from prospects, friends, customers, and the broader market.

## Observation: People are unsure who to trust in AI.

People are not only unsure which tools to use. They are unsure which sources are worth following.

There are many podcasters, YouTubers, newsletters, vendors, tools, opinions, and experts.

This creates decision fatigue.

## Implication

Hacks-a-Thon should position itself as a clear, guided starting point for people and organizations who know AI matters but feel overwhelmed by the noise.

## Observation: Companies tell people to use AI without giving them a useful starting point.

A common message employees hear is: use AI to find efficiencies.

That sounds reasonable, but many employees do not know what that means in practice.

## Implication

The problem is not lack of interest. The problem is lack of clarity.

Hacks-a-Thon helps replace vague encouragement with a structured, motivating experience.
