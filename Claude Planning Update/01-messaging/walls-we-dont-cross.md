# Brand Principles

**Status:** Locked  
**Version:** 1.0

These principles define how Hacks-a-Thon talks about AI, creativity, and organizational change.

## AI is a creative partner.

AI helps people explore, create, and bring ideas to life.

## Every employee can become a creator.

Technical experience should not determine who gets to solve problems.

## Ideas come first.

Technology amplifies ideas.

## Confidence comes from creating.

People gain confidence by making something real.

## AI adoption is a people challenge.

Technology is only successful when people confidently use it.

## Fun matters.

People experiment more, collaborate more, learn more, and continue creating when the experience is enjoyable.

Fun is one of the reasons the methodology works.

## Culture is built through shared creation.

Culture is built when people create together, share what they made, and experience accomplishment as a team.
