# Core Messaging Principles

**Status:** Living  
**Purpose:** Capture the core beliefs and repeatable ideas that should shape Hacks-a-Thon messaging, website copy, sales language, content, and product storytelling.

## 1. Companies don't adopt AI. People do.

AI adoption is a human challenge before it is a technology challenge.

A company can buy tools, approve budgets, and announce initiatives, but adoption only happens when people feel confident enough to use AI in their real work.

## 2. People don't become confident by watching someone else use AI.

They become confident by creating.

Hacks-a-Thon is built around active creation, not passive learning. People need a real experience where they create something themselves and feel what is possible.

## 3. The fastest way to create AI adoption is to first create AI confidence.

Confidence is the gateway to adoption.

When employees believe they can create with AI, they begin using it more naturally, more often, and with more purpose.

## 4. People build confidence fastest when they create something they genuinely care about.

Rather than immediately asking participants to solve company problems, Hacks-a-Thon encourages them to begin with something personally meaningful.

That first success changes how they think about what they can do with AI.

Soon they begin seeing opportunities to use AI everywhere.

## 5. The first successful experience with AI changes how people think about themselves.

The transformation is not simply: AI can do amazing things.

The transformation is: I can create amazing things with AI.

That mindset shift is what carries forward after the program ends.

## 6. Personal creation becomes the gateway to business problem solving.

Participants may begin with an idea that matters to them personally.

Once they experience the confidence of creating with AI, they naturally begin seeing opportunities to solve problems inside their teams, departments, and companies.

## 7. Culture is built through shared creation.

Shared creation leads to shared accomplishment.

Shared accomplishment builds culture.

Culture accelerates AI adoption.
