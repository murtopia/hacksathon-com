# Moments That Matter

**Status:** Research  
**Purpose:** Capture memorable emotional moments from the Hacks-a-Thon experience. These moments may become future stories, keynote material, website copy, or case study material.

## Moments

### The first working creation

The moment a participant realizes their idea is actually working.

This is often when the identity shift begins.

### The Shark Tank presentations

The pitch presentations created energy, laughter, and shared celebration.

They were not only demos. They were culture-building moments.

### The moment someone says, "I made this."

This is the emotional center of the experience.

It marks the transition from learning about AI to believing they can create with AI.

### The first post-event tool

After the Seven2 event, employees built new tools without being asked.

This is proof that the experience created lasting behavior change.

### The ongoing team conversations

Seven2 employees now talk collaboratively about what they can create using AI and AI-powered tools.

That is evidence of culture change.
