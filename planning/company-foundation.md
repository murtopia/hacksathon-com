# Company Foundation

**Status:** Locked  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the foundational principles of Hacks-a-Thon. Every marketing page, sales conversation, product decision, and future offering should align with these principles.

## Mission

We help people realize they can create solutions they never thought possible.

## Vision

We envision organizations where every employee feels empowered to create solutions.

## Category

**Organizational AI Adoption**

Hacks-a-Thon exists to help organizations accelerate AI adoption by empowering every employee to confidently create practical solutions to real business problems.

## Desired Outcome

We help organizations build a culture of AI-enabled problem solving.

A culture where employees do not wait for change. They create it.

## Core Beliefs

### Organizations don't adopt AI. People do.

Successful AI adoption begins with people, not technology.

### AI adoption isn't something you roll out. It's something you cultivate.

Confidence, experimentation, and repeated success create lasting adoption.

### People become confident by creating.

Real confidence comes from solving meaningful problems, not watching demonstrations.

### Every employee can become a creator.

AI has fundamentally changed who gets to create solutions. Innovation is no longer limited to technical teams.

### Innovation already exists inside every organization.

The people closest to the work often have the best ideas. Our role is to help unlock their confidence and their superpowers.

### AI is a creative partner.

We believe the best ideas still begin with people. AI expands what's possible by helping people explore, create, and bring ideas to life faster than ever before.

The future is humans and AI creating together.

## The Promise

When people discover they can create solutions they never thought possible, organizations change because people want to keep creating.
