# Participant Journey

**Status:** Locked  
**Version:** 1.0

## Before

"I know AI is important, but I don't know where to start."

AI feels exciting, but also intimidating.

There are too many tools, too many opinions, too many experts, and too many possible starting points.

The blank prompt is overwhelming.

Building apps feels like something only developers can do.

## Inspiration

"What if I could actually create something?"

Participants begin exploring ideas.

Some solve a problem at work.

Others build something they've always wished existed.

The important part isn't the idea.

It's that the idea matters to them.

## Creation

"I can't believe I built this."

With the right guidance and tools, participants bring their idea to life.

For many, it's the first time they've ever created software, a website, an automation, or another digital tool.

They don't just learn about AI.

They use AI to create something real.

## Confidence

"If I built this, what else could I build?"

The biggest transformation isn't the project.

It's the mindset.

The blank prompt is no longer intimidating.

Participants leave knowing they can create solutions they never thought possible.

## Momentum

Participants continue creating.

Some build tools for themselves.

Some build tools for their teams.

Some solve business problems.

Some create entirely new ideas.

The important part is that they keep creating.

That's when AI adoption becomes real.
