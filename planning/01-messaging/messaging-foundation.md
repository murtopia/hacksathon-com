# Messaging - Foundation

Status: Approved v1.0

## The Business We're In

Hacks-a-Thon exists to help organizations accelerate AI adoption by empowering every employee to confidently create solutions.

## Category

Organizational AI Adoption

## Vision

We envision organizations where every employee feels empowered to create solutions.

## Core Beliefs

- Organizations don't adopt AI. People do.
- AI adoption isn't something you roll out. It's something you cultivate.
- People become confident by creating.
- Every employee can become a creator.
- Innovation already exists inside the organization.

## Notes

This section is foundational. Wording can evolve, but the beliefs should stay protected unless customer learning gives us a reason to change them.
