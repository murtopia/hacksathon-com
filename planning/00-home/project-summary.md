## Current Positioning

Hacks-a-Thon helps organizations accelerate AI adoption by empowering every employee to confidently create solutions.

## Mission

We help people realize they can create solutions they never thought possible.

## Vision

We envision organizations where every employee feels empowered to create solutions.

## Category

Organizational AI Adoption

## Desired Outcome

A culture of AI-enabled problem solving.

## Core Beliefs

- Organizations don't adopt AI. People do.
- AI adoption is something you cultivate.
- People become confident by creating.
- Every employee can become a creator.
- Innovation already exists inside every organization.
- AI is a creative partner.
- Fun matters.
- Culture is built through shared creation.

## Strategic Thesis

Shared creation leads to shared accomplishment.

Shared accomplishment builds culture.

Culture accelerates AI adoption.
