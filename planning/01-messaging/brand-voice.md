# Brand Voice

Status: Draft

## Voice Direction

Human. Founder-led. Clear. Confident but not overhyped.

## Words We Like

- confidence
- create
- solutions
- cultivate
- adoption
- practical
- useful
- momentum
- real business problems

## Words and Phrases to Avoid

- game changer
- unlock your potential
- the future is here
- let's unpack this
- at the end of the day
- low-hanging fruit
- best in class
- make no mistake
- hidden tax
- this changes everything
- excessive em dashes
