# Proof

**Status:** Research  
**Purpose:** Capture real evidence that supports the Hacks-a-Thon methodology and positioning.

## Seven2 Internal Hacks-a-Thon

The Seven2 Hacks-a-Thon proved the methodology internally before turning it into a product.

## Evidence to Capture

### Employees continued creating after the event.

After the Hacks-a-Thon, multiple employees created new tools for Seven2 without being asked.

This is one of the strongest signals of actual AI adoption because behavior continued after the event ended.

### Non-technical employees created useful projects.

The experience helped non-technical employees realize they could create software, websites, automations, and digital tools.

### The Shark Tank pitches were culturally energizing.

The Shark Tank-style presentations were some of the most fun Zoom calls we had as a company.

They created shared accomplishment, celebration, and excitement.

### AI conversations continued after the event.

Seven2 employees now collaboratively talk about what they can create using AI and AI-powered tools.

That is a sign of culture change.

## Proof Thesis

The strongest proof of Hacks-a-Thon is not only what participants built during the event.

It is what they continued creating afterward.
